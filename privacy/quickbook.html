QuickBook Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 November 2026

QuickBook Pilot Checklists is an aircraft checklist app made by Jim Rooney. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use QuickBook on Android, Wear OS, iPad or the web.

What I collect

Nothing. QuickBook has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and it doesn't ask for your location, contacts or any other personal information.

Data stored on your device

The checklists are built into the app. Your selected aircraft, section and checklist line are saved on your device so you keep your place. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Services the app connects to

  • Checklist updates. When you tap Update, the app downloads the latest checklists from a Google Sheets spreadsheet. Google sees your IP address, as with any web request, and handles it under the Google Privacy Policy. Nothing about you is sent with the request.
  • Watch sync. On Android, the phone and a paired Wear OS watch share the current aircraft, section and checklist line through Google Play services so they stay in step. This stays between your own devices.

Children

QuickBook is intended for pilots and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
