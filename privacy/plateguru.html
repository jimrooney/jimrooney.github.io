PlateGuru Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 November 2026

PlateGuru is an app for viewing FAA terminal procedure plates and aeronautical charts. It is made by Jim Rooney. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use PlateGuru on Android or iPad.

What I collect

Nothing. PlateGuru has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. I don't receive your location, your searches or anything else about how you use the app.

Your location

If you allow location access, PlateGuru uses your position on your device to centre the map, draw the 10 NM ring, list nearby airports, show your aircraft on approach plates and choose which charts to download around you. Your position is not sent to me or stored anywhere off your device. If you connect a GDL 90 receiver or the PlateGuru Sim Bridge, that data stays on your local Wi-Fi network.

Services the app connects to

  • FAA servers. Plates, charts, airspace and airport data are downloaded from the FAA. Like any website, those servers see your IP address and which plates or map areas are requested.
  • Google Maps. The map uses the Google Maps SDK, which sends Google device and app diagnostics, crash reports, a pseudonymous SDK identifier and map interaction events such as panning and zooming. Google handles that data under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • App updates. The app may check for and download updates to its own code and plate data. These requests don't include personal information.

Data stored on your device

Downloaded plates and charts, pinned airports and your settings are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Children

PlateGuru is intended for pilots and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
